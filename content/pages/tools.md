## Intro

The OpenSaaSLabs directory is built for practical evaluation. Instead of giving you a generic list of software, every tool profile is designed to answer the questions teams ask before adoption: What does it replace? How mature is it? Can we self-host it? What does it require operationally? What are the risks?
